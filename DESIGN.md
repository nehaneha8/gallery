# Art Portfolio — Creative Direction

## Core idea

This website should feel like entering a small, intimate,
slightly magical gallery that exists inside my personal world.

It should NOT feel like a traditional portfolio website.

The visitor should feel physically present in the environment, should be eye level

also i dont want the website too realistic, it should be like slightly cartoony in the sense that its obviously not a real hallway

## Emotional tone

Warm
Quiet
Dreamlike
Intimate
Handmade
Slightly mysterious
Nostalgic

Not:
- futuristic
- cyberpunk
- corporate
- overly polished
- bright white gallery
- video-game HUD

## Environment

The main environment is a narrow wooden hallway.

The hallway contains:
- warm wooden flooring
- softly textured walls
- framed paintings
- candles
- hanging/wall lanterns
- subtle fireflies
- floating dust
- warm pools of amber light
- darker areas between lights

## Camera

Camera represents a person standing in the hallway.

Approximate eye level:
1.6m

Movement should feel calm and deliberate.

The user does NOT freely fly around.

Movement happens primarily through glowing interaction points.

## Artwork interaction

Paintings hang naturally on the hallway walls.

When a painting is hovered:
- subtle glow appears
- cursor indicates interaction
- perhaps the frame becomes slightly illuminated

When clicked:
- camera smoothly moves toward painting
- camera centers itself directly in front
- surrounding environment becomes less visually dominant

There should be an option for the user can return to the hallway.

## Movement interaction

Small glowing points appear near the floor.

Clicking a point smoothly moves the camera to that location.

The movement should resemble walking rather than teleportation.

## Bedroom

A wooden door appears near the end of the hallway.

The door can be clicked.

The door physically swings open.

The user moves through the doorway.

Behind it is a recreation of my bedroom with the graffiti wall

The graffiti wall should be based directly on supplied reference photographs.

## Atmosphere

Fireflies/glow dots should move slowly and irregularly.

They should be subtle.

Never more than approximately 50 visible at once.

Candle flames should flicker gently.

Nothing should feel chaotic.

## Visual priorities

1. Artwork
2. Atmosphere
3. Environment
4. Interaction indicators
5. UI

Traditional UI should be extremely minimal.
